隐私说明

  • 注册只需要邮箱，平台不保存任何密码，也不需要信用卡。
  • 试用期为邮箱验证通过起 72 小时。到期、提前结束或被管理员删除时，工作区数据打包为一个 ZIP，存放在 Backblaze B2 对象存储（服务端加密），下载链接与「立即删除」链接只通过邮件发送一次，7 天有效；7 天后 ZIP 永久删除。
  • 邮件发出后，业务数据、账户、邮箱与会话立即删除；平台备份与运行日志在 7 天内自然过期。邮件服务商（Resend / Brevo）按其保留期保存投递记录，已关闭点击与打开追踪。
  • 前端不使用任何第三方统计脚本，只加载 Cloudflare Turnstile 人机验证组件。
  • 平台管理员可以查看任何数据并对任何账户执行操作，所有管理员操作写入不可修改的审计日志。

Privacy notice

  • Sign-up needs only an e-mail address; no passwords are stored and no credit card is needed.
  • The trial lasts 72 hours from e-mail verification. When it ends (expiry, early termination or admin deletion) the workspace data is packed into one ZIP in Backblaze B2 object storage (server-side encrypted). The download link and a "delete now" link are e-mailed once and are valid for 7 days; then the ZIP is deleted for good.
  • Once the e-mail is sent, business data, account, e-mail and sessions are deleted at once; platform backups and runtime logs expire within 7 days. The e-mail providers (Resend / Brevo) keep delivery records per their retention; click and open tracking is disabled.
  • The web app loads no third-party analytics, only the Cloudflare Turnstile human check.
  • The platform admin can see any data and act on any account; every admin action is written to an append-only audit log.

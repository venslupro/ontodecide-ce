隐私说明

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日

本说明介绍 OntoDecide 社区版（以下简称“本平台”）在你注册和使用试用账户时，如何收集、使用、保存和删除你的个人信息。请在注册前完整阅读；同意本说明后方可注册。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 邮箱地址：用于注册、发送登录验证码和试用状态通知。
  • 你在工作区中导入或创建的业务数据。
  • 保障安全所必需的技术信息：IP 地址、浏览器类型、时区与请求日志。
  • 我们不收集密码、手机号、身份证件或任何支付信息。

2. 信息的使用

  • 提供试用服务：创建工作区、运行分析与推荐。
  • 发送验证码、试用即将到期和试用结束（含数据下载链接）的邮件。
  • 防止滥用与排查故障：人机验证、频率限制和运行日志。
  • 不用于广告，不出售、不出租给任何第三方。

3. 保存期限与删除

  • 试用账户有效期为 3 天：自邮箱验证通过起 72 小时。
  • 试用到期、提前结束或被管理员删除时，工作区数据打包为一个 ZIP（服务端加密存储）；下载链接与“立即删除”链接只通过邮件发送一次，7 天内有效，之后 ZIP 永久删除。
  • 邮件发出后，业务数据、账户、邮箱与会话立即删除；平台备份与运行日志在 7 天内自然过期。

4. 第三方服务

  • Cloudflare：网站托管、网络防护与 Turnstile 人机验证。
  • Backblaze B2：试用结束后的 ZIP 归档存储。
  • Resend：邮件投递，按其保留期保存投递记录；已关闭点击与打开追踪。
  • 网页不加载任何第三方统计或广告脚本。

5. Cookie 与本地存储

  • 仅使用登录所必需的会话 Cookie（HttpOnly、Secure），不使用追踪 Cookie。
  • 界面语言、主题等偏好只保存在你的浏览器本地。

6. 安全与管理员访问

  • 所有传输均通过 HTTPS 加密。
  • 平台管理员可以查看任何数据并对任何账户执行操作；所有管理员操作写入不可修改的审计日志。

7. 你的权利

  • 可随时在“账户”页提前结束试用，数据按第 3 条立即处理。
  • 可通过邮件中的“立即删除”链接提前删除归档。
  • 可在“账户”页查看并修改界面语言与时区。

8. 本说明的变更

  • 本说明如有变更，会更新上方的生效日期；重大变更会在注册和登录页提示。

Privacy notice

Effective 1 October 2026

This notice explains how OntoDecide Community Edition ("the platform") collects, uses, keeps and deletes your personal data when you sign up for and use a trial account. Please read it in full before signing up; you must accept it to create an account.

1. What we collect

  • Your e-mail address: for sign-up, sign-in codes and trial notifications.
  • The business data you import into or create in your workspace.
  • Technical data needed for security: IP address, browser type, time zone and request logs.
  • We never collect passwords, phone numbers, ID documents or payment details.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the trial: create your workspace, run analyses and recommendations.
  • To e-mail sign-in codes, trial expiry reminders and the trial-ended message with your data download link.
  • To prevent abuse and fix faults: human checks, rate limits and runtime logs.
  • Never for advertising; never sold or rented to anyone.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Trial accounts are valid for 3 days: 72 hours from e-mail verification.
  • When the trial ends (expiry, early termination or admin deletion) the workspace data is packed into one ZIP (encrypted at rest). The download link and a "delete now" link are e-mailed once and are valid for 7 days; then the ZIP is deleted for good.
  • Once that e-mail is sent, business data, account, e-mail and sessions are deleted at once; platform backups and runtime logs expire within 7 days.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare: hosting, network protection and the Turnstile human check.
  • Backblaze B2: storage of the ZIP archive after the trial.
  • Resend: e-mail delivery; delivery records are kept per its retention; click and open tracking is disabled.
  • The web app loads no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

5. Cookies and local storage

  • Only the session cookie needed to stay signed in (HttpOnly, Secure); no tracking cookies.
  • Preferences such as language and theme are kept in your browser only.

6. Security and admin access

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The platform admin can see any data and act on any account; every admin action is written to an append-only audit log.

7. Your rights

  • End the trial early at any time on the Account page; your data is then handled as in section 3.
  • Delete the archive early with the "delete now" link in the e-mail.
  • View and change your language and time zone on the Account page.

8. Changes to this notice

  • If this notice changes we update the effective date above; material changes are announced on the sign-up and sign-in pages.
